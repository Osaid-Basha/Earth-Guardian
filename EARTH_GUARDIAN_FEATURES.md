# Earth Guardian
## Smart Integrated Disaster Management System

### 🌍 Overview
Earth Guardian is a smart integrated system that connects space data with people's daily lives. What satellites monitor from rain, fires, earthquakes, and high waves is directly transformed into clear warnings and practical action plans on Earth.

### 📱 Citizen-Focused Features

#### 🚨 Early Warning System
- **Advanced Alerts**: Up to 48 hours ahead with confidence levels (e.g., "82% flood probability within 36 hours")
- **Multi-Channel Notifications**: Push, SMS, WhatsApp, and local sirens
- **Clear Messages**: "Flood in 12 hours - prepare for evacuation"

#### 🗺️ Dynamic Evacuation Map
- **Live Map**: Shows safe routes in real-time
- **Google Maps Integration**: With voice guidance during movement
- **Multiple Layers**: Danger zones, shelters, safe routes

#### 🆘 SOS Button & Advanced Reporting
- **Instant Send**: GPS location and photos/videos
- **AI Classification**: Automatically categorizes reports (fire/flood/collapse)
- **Smart Analysis**: Instant recommendations based on disaster type

#### 🎓 Interactive Education Library
- **Educational Videos**: Short, practical tips
- **Interactive Scenarios**: "What to do during earthquake/flood"
- **Educational Games**: "Survivor Points" system to motivate learning
- **Multiple Categories**: Games, videos, scenarios

#### 🤖 Built-in Smart Assistant
- **Instant Instructions**: Customized by disaster type and user location
- **Voice Commands**: "Where is the nearest shelter?" or "What should I do now?"
- **Offline Mode**: Works without internet with local database
- **Smart Responses**: Based on context and location

#### 📞 Emergency Contacts & Shelters
- **Local Emergency Numbers**: With availability status
- **Nearest Hospitals**: With congestion status
- **Shelter Locations**: And high ground safe areas

#### 📱 Offline Mode
- **Pre-loaded**: Maps and instructions
- **Smart Assistant**: Works without internet
- **Emergency List**: Always available
- **First Aid Guide**: Stored locally

### 🏛️ Government & Official Features

#### 📊 Central Control Dashboard
- **Interactive Map**: Displays all warnings and reports
- **Data Layers**: Rain, terrain, roads, community reports
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Updates every 15 minutes

#### ✅ Data Verification
- **Alert Review**: Before public release
- **False Alert Prevention**: Advanced verification system
- **Official Approval**: Before sending notifications

#### 🚨 Field Crisis Management
- **Report Reception**: With photos, videos, and GPS locations
- **Automatic Classification**: Of reports and map display
- **Mass Alert**: Send SMS/Push messages to residents in specific areas

#### 🚛 Resource & Supply Distribution
- **Team Tracking**: Via GPS
- **Route Suggestions**: For assistance even with road closures
- **Warehouse Monitoring**: Water, food, medicine

#### 📈 Analytics & Predictions
- **Warning Indicators**: With confidence levels
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Animated disaster path tracking
- **Post-Disaster Analysis**: Before/after image comparison

#### 🔗 Smart System Integration
- **Alert Systems**: In buildings
- **Metro Stations**: Route changes when needed
- **Radio & TV**: Official warning broadcasts
- **Drones**: Field map updates

#### 🎯 Training & Simulation
- **Simulation Mode**: For rescue team training
- **Virtual Exercises**: For the community
- **Interactive Scenarios**: To increase preparedness
